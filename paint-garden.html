Paint Garden

How a match works

  • Host opens Start Match on the laptop and shares the 4-letter code.
  • Up to 10 players open Join Match on their phones, enter a name, and pick a color on the wheel. Colors are unique — no duplicates.
  • Hold the paint button to spray a liquid stream that arcs where you're moving; a quick tap lobs a single paint ball worth 3 units. Your paint supply holds 100 units: the stream drains 3 per second, a ball costs 3, and the supply refills at 1 per second. Empty means no spraying until it refills.
  • Spray paint into the flower pots to grow plants. A full flower (30 units) pays 20 points to its biggest contributor. A pot nobody has started fades away after 15 seconds — once any paint lands in it, it stays.
  • Spray other jellyfish to turn them your color. At 100 units they join your team as a servant — their banked points are wiped and everything they earn goes to you.
  • First team to 500 points wins, or the highest score when the 10-minute clock runs out.
